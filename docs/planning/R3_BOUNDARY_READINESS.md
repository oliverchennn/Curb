# R3 evidence snapshot readiness

Decision packet for `R3-BOUNDARY-PREP/1`, based on `c952d7de0328bb31ba5f677b9800684b2c6366b8`. The proposed first R3 checkpoint is a read-only, versioned local snapshot for one Princeton corridor. A reviewer can inspect a crossing end, path segment or entrance, see which evidence supports the attachment, and see where current conditions remain unknown. Field collection, publication rights and implementation still need their own decisions. [R3 slice](../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#slice-r3-replace-the-illustrative-map-with-real-evidence) · [Readiness assignment](../tasks/READINESS_QUEUE.md#r3-boundary-prep--1--existing-a).

## Source evidence available for selection

The pinned [research ZIP](../source/Curb_Research_and_Data.zip) hashes to
`a1757bb7c964f9e6f4e178b86742b3994e299d7c1b2af67de220f4c445d43475`. The hash and feature count of each of its four GeoJSON members match `sources.json`. Central Princeton has 148 path/crossing lines and 83 ramp points, all tagged `AERIAL`. The lines omit width, cross-slope and condition dates. Ramp points have no ADA or detectable-warning fields populated. Preserve `EXISTING` (54 points) and `ASSUMED` (29) as source statuses with attribution. The “as of” date describes the extract. Per-feature capture dates and actual field dates remain unknown. The wider `MISSING` record 491033 is an aerial scouting lead outside the central extract. The 48 Pittsburgh clusters are separate historical imagery research. [Research checkpoint](https://github.com/vzhu08/Curb/issues/1#issuecomment-6873427490).

The published [Princeton scout packet](https://github.com/vzhu08/Curb/blob/2d56e6a0a2ca4e70fd2977009d572e983182fb4c/docs/research/PRINCETON_CORRIDOR.md) proposes 18 stops, including both ends of the primary and alternate crossings, approach links, and the library, gate and campus entrance targets. Its archive coordinate associations support field planning. The packet records no field verification, with unlocated entrances and unverified links still open. Use its stable stop IDs as collection references and confirm the exact transition or marked side on site. Draw only defensible geometry, and leave an unverified gap disconnected or explicitly unresolved. [Coordinator verification](https://github.com/vzhu08/Curb/issues/1#issuecomment-6876885700).

## Proposed version 1 boundary

Use `EvidenceSnapshotSchema` with `contractVersion: "curb-r3"`, `schemaVersion: 1`, and immutable identity `{ snapshotId, revision }`. Store authored graph, evidence, attachments, coverage and asset metadata arrays in the same snapshot. Reuse `NodeSchema`, `EdgeSchema`, `DestinationSchema` and the compatible barrier shape for geometry and references. Keep the existing types with their current semantics when used by Foundation, R1 and R2. Validate the new snapshot's references directly, including source/record IDs, unique IDs, edge endpoints, destination nodes, reciprocal barrier links, and evidence attachment ownership. A provenance record can attach to an edge even when there is no identified barrier.

| Proposed record | Fields and validation |
| --- | --- |
| `ArchiveSource` | `id`, `sourceKind: "aerial_archive" \| "archive_record"`, provider and dataset/layer, pinned source URL, artifact reference and SHA-256, required attribution and reviewed terms reference, `retrievedAt` and optional `sourceAsOf`. Use `aerial_archive` for the DVRPC extract. Source links may be opened deliberately; the app loads data and media locally. |
| `ArchiveEvidence` | Local `id`, `sourceId`, `sourceRecordId`, literal caption/note, and source status where available. The upstream key is source snapshot + dataset/layer + original feature ID. Record `capturedAt` for feature-specific source imagery, `sourceRecordedAt` for a source label/record date if supplied, and keep Curb observation time in the field variant. Aggregate capture dates or votes can stay in audit notes and do not stand in for a dated capture or field observation. |
| `FieldEvidence` | Local `id`, `sourceKind: "field_observation"`, `fieldRecordId`, literal caption/note, `observedAt` and optional photo metadata with its own `capturedAt`. Tie the note to actual human observation of a named location and stated property. Permit an attributable retained record with unknown time if the original supports the field provenance. Carry widths, slopes or rises only when an actual measurement and method are available. |
| `EvidenceAttachment` | `evidenceId`, reviewed target `{ edgeId, barrierId? }` or `null` for an unresolved location, and `review: { status: "pending" \| "accepted" \| "rejected", reviewedAt, note? }`. Accepted/rejected attachments require a reason and a valid target when accepted. An accepted attachment means the evidence supports the link and caption within its scope. Evidence with a different date or conclusion stays independently visible. |
| `CoverageRecord` | One record per edge, with `coverage: "uninspected" \| "partial" \| "inspected"`, linked field evidence IDs and a reason. Coverage describes which conditions were inspected. Display current traversal as unknown in this read-only version, including on inspected segments. A future traversal decision would need its own authored profile assessment and links for each required transition. |

Use a shared date value of `null`, `{ date: "YYYY-MM-DD" }`, or `{ timestamp: ISO8601WithOffset }` so explicit unknown and original precision survive parsing. The verified archive retrieval date belongs to the source. Preserve a manifest's as-of date as `sourceAsOf` when retrieval time is unverified. Archive capture/record dates and field observation/photo dates belong to their records. Review has its own date. Show unknown where a date applies and omit inapplicable dates, such as a field observation date on an archive record. Date-only records never get an invented clock time. Establish how legacy field records qualify for migration before adding any; v1 fixtures keep their existing values and rendering.

Include a photo only with cleared publication rights, a caption and a validated local path under `/curb-evidence/<snapshotId>/<revision>/`. List each published asset with SHA-256 in the snapshot and check disk existence, bytes and resolved containment offline or server-side. Runtime validation rejects remote URLs, traversal, ambiguous encodings and cross-snapshot paths. An unavailable photo can remain a note with an explicit media status. Keep original photos, incidental personal information and field notes outside the public tree pending review.

## Acceptance if selected

1. Parse the existing sample, empty, trip and plan fixtures against their frozen schemas. Confirm their selectors, response envelopes, revisions and golden named outcomes still behave as before. Check that R1/R2 reject the new snapshot and any R3 ID supplied to their selectors.
2. Load one valid mixed snapshot. A candidate aerial ramp without a capture date shows source status, attribution, retrieval date/precision, “Capture date unknown” and unknown current traversal. A later actual field record shows its own observation date and optional independent image capture date. Accepted archive attachment, pending field attachment and conflicting statements leave current traversal unknown.
3. Reject duplicate IDs, orphan source, missing edge/destination, cross-edge barrier attachment, unsupported version, mismatched snapshot identity, invalid date shapes and disallowed media paths. Deliberately unresolved attachments remain browseable with no implied target or traversal.
4. Verify a crossing with two independently linked transitions, an unknown opposite side, an unsurveyed alternate and an unresolved entrance. Absence of an observed barrier, a coordinate match or a reviewed photo alone must never turn coverage into a pass.
5. Exercise a valid GET, bad/unknown selector, corrupt fixture, malformed/wrong-identity response, empty evidence and aborted load. A missing approved local image retains the caption and shows an unavailable message. Check keyboard selection, accessible names and status, and no horizontal overflow at 390 px. Verify no provider requests in the browsing flow.
6. Record actual fixture/media hashes, tests and browser observations at the implementation SHA. Identify which evidence was directly observed and which spans remain unknown. Record selected R1/R2 fixture regression checks separately from any field inspection.

## Decision required

Confirm the first corridor extent and inspected profile properties after collecting field notes, including both crossing transitions, entrances and alternates. Confirm publication and attribution per source/photo, reviewer identity policy, and the review rule for stale, temporary or conflicting observations. Decide whether the first release should publish archive-only scouting with explicit unknown coverage if field records are unavailable. Recommend the read-only evidence checkpoint first. Keep repair outcome comparison on the labeled synthetic R2 case until field coverage and cost evidence support a separately approved extension.
