# Human study protocol v1

This protocol measures the time and correctness of a recommendation and brief on one synthetic campus funding task after setup. It fixes design and analysis before recruiting. The synthetic strategy benchmark has its own preregistration.

- Randomly assign 6 or 8 convenience-sample participants to one attempt each. Both arms use the same versioned packet, CSV, budget, information, clock, orientation, answer template, and allowed pencil/paper/calculator/spreadsheet. Curb arm also gets the pinned, accepted Investment demo. Exclude Curb authors and anyone who has already seen this exercise's answer, demo, or rubric. Dry runs are separate. Facilities, student, and other are anonymous role categories.
- Before recruitment, choose 6 or 8 slots and freeze the allocation receipt. Seed 20261115 initializes unsigned xorshift32 (left 13, logical right 17, left 5). Fisher–Yates shuffles a list with the first half Manual and second half Curb. At index i, swap with floor(unsigned draw / 2^32 × (i+1)). Reserve S01 to S06 or S08 in order and assign the next slot after consent and eligibility. Keep upcoming assignments and the rubric hidden from participants. No substitutions after a slot is assigned and no resizing after recruitment starts.
- Use scenario `synthetic-campus-investment@1/packet-v1` at $10,000 with equal destination weights and one authored profile and origin. The facilitator records accepted app source, environment, URL, and acceptance evidence even for Manual rows, which reference the same study setup. Capture and freeze the packet, CSV, rubric, template, and accepted app receipt. Recheck the app revision each Curb session. If the app or protocol changes materially, suspend this cohort or open a distinct version; never pool mismatched versions.
- Time begins when the participant can see the prepared inputs and open the assigned task tool. Include reading, reasoning, evidence review, writing, and final handoff; stop when the final shared-format brief is submitted. Fixed wall-clock cap of 600 seconds. Log technical interruptions without pausing or removing time. The resulting time excludes recruitment, device setup, app preflight, field inspection, data collection and preparation, modeling, and ingestion. Record staff setup effort separately in the observer record when possible.
- Keep all frozen slots and distinguish unfilled slots, no shows, prestart withdrawals, final submissions, timeouts, abandonments, and irrecoverable technical failures. Timeout is right-censored at 600 seconds and is never a completed or successful submission. An assignment mismatch is recorded against the original assigned arm. Record substantive hints and interruptions with contemporaneous notes. Participants can stop at any time; retain only authorized evidence. If required proof is unavailable, keep the disposition in facilitator accounting and report analysis as incomplete.
- The first scorer uses the frozen six-item rubric on every final submission, blinded to arm where possible. A second independent reviewer checks every score and resolves disagreements before analysis. Full correctness requires all six passes and no flagged error. Incorrect final submissions count as completed attempts but not full successes.
- Group by assigned arm. Report all slot and enrollment denominators, started and completed counts, fully correct counts/rates, each rubric and error count, raw observed durations and status, and median/range for completed and for fully correct submissions. Keep timeouts, interrupted/hinted sessions, withdrawals, and crossovers visible. The predeclared unassisted subset is final fully correct submissions with no hints or interruptions and actual arm matching assignment. Compute percentage difference as 100 × (Manual median − Curb median) / Manual median only with at least three such submissions per assigned arm. Report both medians and denominators and the overall completion, correctness, and error rates beside any percentage. If Curb had lower completion/correctness or a higher scored error rate, lead with the tradeoff. Report unfavorable outcomes and zero differences.
- Interpret results as descriptive task evidence for this sample and version. Participants may recognize the extra tool and the test does not generalize to campus populations, actual construction results, or end-to-end operational savings. The allocation and proof gate cannot independently establish participant identity or factual conduct. Human observers and reviewers attest to the records; publish only authorized anonymized artifacts.

The [facilitator guide](HUMAN_STUDY_GUIDE.md) gives the collection procedure and commands. A versioned kit alone is `study_ready / observations_pending`. Only verified input with actual human task attempts can be `study_observed`; publication and headline review remain separate.
