This is the author-proposed `historical-source-inventory-v1` policy. It is not an accepted routing policy, mobility profile or legal verdict. Independent review is pending.

Known recorded barriers remain in the inventory even when route attribution, costs or dimensions are unresolved. Source priority and featured barrier fields do not replace the full ramp feature tables. Construction414 and maintenance415 are separately typed and remain outside repair candidates. Summary totals are contextual records, not additional physical projects.

There are no strategy outputs, benefit estimates, people-helped metrics, current access claims, arbitrary optimistic edges, synthetic-fixture substitutions or approved presenter headlines in this package. A future scoped comparison would require a reviewed input/policy freeze and a new explicit assignment.
