## Verified behavior and scope

- The export workflow link and **Download PDF** are keyboard-reachable with visible focus in Chrome. Enter started the PDF request; Space retried successfully after the injected error. **Download JSON** and **Download CSV** also worked by keyboard without adding a PDF POST. No page errors occurred. The pre-request, working, failure and success states were observed in the browser. The 500 was injected once; the retry used the real endpoint.

Plain extraction gave comparison, work areas, evidence 01–05 and sources in order. No manual screen reader, PDF assistive-technology interoperability, or PDF/UA certification check was performed.
