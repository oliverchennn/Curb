# Robertson Hall presenter Q&A

The answers describe the merged Robertson release and accompany the two-minute demo. [Demo script](ROBERTSON_DEMO_SCRIPT.md), [Merged release](https://github.com/oliverchennn/Curb/commit/3bcc6f63e49eae883a428c505421851db0125718).

**Why start with disability access?** The same uneven edge may affect a small wheelchair wheel, a walker, a cane tip or footing in different ways. Curb gives facilities a way to review the footage, record what needs checking and compare repair scopes while keeping unresolved access needs visible. [Finding review and inspection checklist](../../src/features/curb/survey/FindingReview.tsx#L71), [Demo rationale](ROBERTSON_DEMO_SCRIPT.md#two-minute-script).

**What does this video tell us about the sidewalk?** This is an actual 14-second phone clip supplied as Robertson Hall footage. It shows visible cracking and surface breakup. Each proposal links to a close-up and a time, including the final crack at frame 418. Opening width and orientation, level difference, edge stability, remaining clear width and slopes still require field checks. Route impact and ADA compliance remain undetermined. [Media provenance](../../public/curb/surveys/robertson-hall/README.md), [Survey evidence and unresolved measurements](../../src/data/curb/surveys/robertson-hall.json#L92), [Contract for route status](../../src/contracts/curb/survey.ts#L57).

**Where does AI run?** During preparation, AI reviewed contact sheets and full-resolution frames to propose findings and reference boxes. A separate optical-flow step followed nearby visual features between frames, checked them in both directions and rejected outliers. Reviewed reference boxes helped correct drift. During the demo, the app loads the saved survey and displays boxes for retained frames. It makes no live model calls. [Proposal provenance](../../src/data/curb/surveys/robertson-analysis.json#L2), [Tracking code](../../scripts/curb/process_video.py#L44), [Survey loader](../../src/server/curb/survey.ts#L29), [Exact-frame lookup](../../src/domain/curb/survey.ts#L88).

**Why five proposals, and how did you distinguish joints?** The inventory includes irregular cracks and connected breakup visible in this clip. Ordinary straight control joints were excluded. A later review added the connected breakup and the final transverse crack after the user pointed them out. The five proposals await field review. A reviewer can accept, reject or correct one. A rejected finding stays in the report and is excluded from plan selection. When a track drops out, visibility is uncertain. [Proposal notes on joints](../../src/data/curb/surveys/robertson-analysis.json#L6), [Inventory revision](../../src/data/curb/surveys/robertson-analysis.json#L159), [Review controls](../../src/features/curb/survey/FindingReview.tsx#L98), [Playback note](../../src/features/curb/survey/VideoEvidence.tsx#L139).

**What does the map establish? Could aerial or archive imagery replace the video?** The map shows nearby OpenStreetMap buildings, streets and paths, plus one approximate phone GPS point for the clip. The five findings have no confirmed map locations. Aerial or archived sources can help decide where to inspect. Close-ups and on-site measurements are needed to assess small gaps, level changes, slope and continuous passage. [Map provenance](../../public/curb/surveys/robertson-hall/README.md#local-map-context), [Site context component](../../src/features/curb/survey/SiteContext.tsx#L28), [Princeton archive limits](../research/PRINCETON_CORRIDOR.md#verified-archive-anchors).

**How does Curb choose repairs?** Essential prioritizes near-term areas and chooses the option with the lowest known upper estimate where available. Balanced uses the catalog's recommended options, and Durable favors the highest-rated durability. Given a budget, Curb considers one priced treatment per area and prioritizes coverage of high-priority areas, then medium, low and unassessed. Deferred needs stay visible. Suitability and provisional priority can be reviewed as more evidence comes in. [Plan selection and tie rules](../../src/domain/curb/survey.ts#L58), [Review controls](../../src/features/curb/survey/FindingReview.tsx#L105).

**Why does a $1,500 budget still show a partial fit?** In the original survey, that budget selects seals for areas 01, 02 and 03. Their known planning subtotal is $216–$1,434. It uses assumed quantities and historical bid references. Shared site verification and setup need one quote for the job. Localized patches also need quotes and are excluded from numeric budget selection. Areas 04 and 05 are deferred in this example. [Budget and quote evidence](ROBERTSON_FIVE_EXPORT_QA.md), [Cost accounting](../../src/domain/curb/survey.ts#L29), [Bid references and limits](ROBERTSON_DEMO_SCRIPT.md#one-page-rehearsal-checklist).

**Why can the library, clinic and pharmacy demo show route changes?** That separate demo uses an authored network and hypothetical repair effects. Its library distance and clinic and pharmacy reachability demonstrate the routing logic on those sample connections. Robertson Hall has no surveyed route graph. Every Robertson plan therefore shows unknown route impact. [Synthetic replay scenario table](../../demo/survey-replay/README.md#curb-survey-replay), [Robertson route impact](../../src/features/curb/survey/PlanCompare.tsx#L75).

**What can I export and use offline?** PDF and accessible HTML show the current review and plan comparison. CSV and JSON carry the survey and review records. The original MOV and fixed annotated MP4 are also downloadable. The saved HTML embeds all five close-ups and opens with the app stopped. Following an external source link requires a connection. Use the HTML for screen-reader use and the untagged PDF for visual reference or print. The installed app runs locally with the loopback server and committed assets. [Export controls](../../src/features/curb/survey/ExportPanel.tsx#L137), [Offline report runbook](ROBERTSON_PORTABLE_RELEASE.md#present-from-the-saved-report).

**What comes next before deployment or work orders?** Ask disabled campus users and facilities staff to try the review, priority, budget and export tasks in voluntary sessions. Then have facilities confirm the site measurements and treatment scope with professional inspection and compare current, itemized quotes. Those steps inform whether to expand the workflow or connect it to work orders. [Pilot validation packet and decision criteria](https://github.com/oliverchennn/Curb/blob/1ff75a3467b5ccf7a2554ce53e1b46cafe457c90/docs/demo/ROBERTSON_PILOT_VALIDATION.md#decide-whether-to-advance).
