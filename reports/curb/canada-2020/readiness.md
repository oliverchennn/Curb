# Cañada College evidence readiness

**Inventory only.** The 2020 San Mateo County Community College District survey supports a historical inventory for the Lot 1 and Building 9 area. The case has 69 reviewed records, including measured features, route summaries and source context. The independent source review is complete. Ten evidence requirements remain open before comparing repairs. [Independent review](../../../docs/research/canada-case/DATA_REVIEW.md)
